About

Blair Willcox, PE

Founder of CurrentTech Energy. About 19 years in energy efficiency, decarbonization, sustainability strategy and renewable energy.

Blair started in industrial energy efficiency, finding and cutting waste at plants, and is a US Department of Energy Qualified Specialist in process heating and compressed air systems. From there the work moved to decarbonization and RE100 strategy for multi-site portfolios, combining efficiency, on-site solar, offsite renewables and supply chain engagement, including engagements led for 15 of the Fortune 200.

That led to renewable procurement: virtual power purchase agreements that supported four deals totaling hundreds of megawatts of wind in ERCOT, SPP and AESO. Contracting at that scale means living inside the wholesale market, its prices, its basis risk and its settlement math.

Years of modeling those prices made one thing obvious. Grids with lots of wind and solar have long stretches of very cheap power, and that power can make heat for less than natural gas. Universities and other big thermal users can capture it with electric steam and hot water added to the plants they already run, without an expensive conversion of their whole distribution system. CurrentTech exists to make that happen, and to get the power contracts right so the savings are real.

Blair speaks on industrial decarbonization and grid-interactive load. Recent talks include "Decarbonizing Heating in Manufacturing and Other Medium-Temperature Applications" and "Renewables and Grid-Supporting Industrial Electrification", comparing electric boilers, steam-generating heat pumps and electric-thermal storage, and the power contracts that make each one work.

The same market work is public at ERCOT Bids, a free explorer of every ERCOT operating day.
